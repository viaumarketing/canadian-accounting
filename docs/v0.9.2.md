# v0.9.2 — QuickBooks Online–style interface, invoices and sales receipts

Status: **implemented and verified** (API tests and an in-browser walk-through); see Results.

## What "looks like QuickBooks Online" means here
The **layout and workflows** familiar to QuickBooks Online users:
- a dark left navigation with a **+ New** button that opens a grid of everything you can create;
- a **Sales** hub with tabs (All sales, Invoices, Sales receipts, Customers, Products & services) and a money bar (overdue, not due yet, paid in the last 30 days);
- full-page invoice and sales-receipt forms with the same fields in the same places;
- a printable customer document.

It does **not** copy Intuit's name, logo, icons or other brand assets: those are trademarks, and the app keeps its own name.

## Plan

### Data (one additive migration)
- **Products & services (`Item`):** name, SKU, type (service / non-inventory), sales description, rate, income account, default tax code, active. Inventory quantity tracking is not included.
- **Sales receipts (`SalesReceipt`, `SalesReceiptLine`):** a sale paid on the spot.
  - Fields: customer, date, payment method, reference no., deposit-to account (a bank or cash account), lines like an invoice, message, memo.
  - Numbered `SR-…` when posted.
  - Posting: **Dr deposit account, Cr revenue, Cr GST/HST/QST/PST** through the same ledger path as invoices.
  - Void reverses the entry.
- **Invoices gain:** terms (Due on receipt, Net 15/30/60), billing address, message on invoice, internal memo, and per line the product/service and a service date.
- **Customers gain:** billing address, phone, default terms.
- **The company gains:** address, phone, e-mail (printed on documents).

### API
- Items: list, create, update, deactivate.
- Sales receipts: list, get, create (draft), post, void.
- **All sales**: one list of invoices, credit notes, sales receipts and customer payments, plus money-bar totals.
- Customer-facing **PDF** for invoices and sales receipts: company details, GST/HST registration number, bill-to, lines, tax by component, totals, balance due.
- Tax reports, Quick Method, schedules and revenue-by-channel include sales receipts. Bank matching sees them automatically, since they post to the bank account.

### Interface
- New theme and shell (navigation groups, **+ New** menu, top bar).
- Sales hub tabs as listed above. The invoice and sales-receipt editors are full pages, with view mode, Print/PDF, receive payment and void.
- Customers: list plus a create/edit side panel. Products & services: list plus a create/edit side panel.

### Not included
Sending documents by e-mail (no mail server is configured), online card payments, inventory tracking, estimates, recurring invoices, "Undeposited Funds" / bank deposits, custom invoice templates and logos.

### Acceptance
- Posting a sales receipt creates a balanced journal: Dr bank, Cr revenue and tax.
- Sales receipts appear in the GST/HST report and its schedule, and their checks stay green.
- The All sales list and money bar total correctly.
- PDFs render.
- The route-permission snapshot is updated deliberately.
- Every new screen is checked in the browser.
- Lint, typecheck and all tests pass, and the migration is additive.

## Results

### Look and feel
- **Shell:** charcoal left navigation with a green **+ New** button. The menu groups Customers (Invoice, Receive payment, Sales receipt, Credit note, Customer), Suppliers (Expense, Bill, Supplier) and Other (Journal entry, Bank statement import, Product or service).
- **Navigation:** sections expand to show their pages (Sales → All sales, Invoices, Sales receipts, Customers, Products & services; Expenses → Expenses, Bills, Suppliers).
- **Top bar:** white, with the company switcher, settings, light/dark toggle and account menu.
- **Theme:** light grey canvas with green primary actions, plus a dark mode.
- **Sales hub:**
  - tabs for All sales, Invoices, Sales receipts, Customers and Products & services;
  - a money bar showing overdue, not due yet, and paid in the last 30 days. Clicking a segment filters the list.
  - the transactions table: Date, Type, No., Customer, Due date, Balance, Total, Status (e.g. "Due in 13 days", "Overdue 10 days", "Paid") and Action (Receive payment / Edit / Print).
- **Invoice / credit note / sales receipt editor** (full page):
  - customer with "+ Add new customer", and the customer's email;
  - billing address, terms (Due on receipt, Net 15/30/60) with the due date calculated, and dates;
  - amounts exclusive or inclusive of tax;
  - line grid: service date, product/service (fills description, rate and tax code), description, qty, rate, amount, sales tax;
  - "Add lines" / "Clear all lines";
  - message on the document and an internal memo;
  - running totals with tax by rate;
  - sticky action bar: Cancel, Save draft, **Save and post** / **Save and record**.
  Once posted it becomes read-only, with Print or download, Receive payment and Void.
- **Customers:** list with open balance and search; create/edit side panel with email, phone, billing address, province and default terms.
- **Products & services:** list plus a side panel (service / non-inventory, SKU, description, price, income account, default sales tax, make inactive).
- **Printable PDF** (letter size):
  - company name, address, phone, e-mail and GST/HST/QST registration numbers;
  - document title and number, dates and terms;
  - Bill to / Sold to;
  - lines with product, description, qty, rate, amount and tax code;
  - subtotal, tax by component and rate, total, payments and balance due;
  - the message;
  - a DRAFT or VOID watermark.

### Walk-through in the browser (dev database, Québec company)
1. **New invoice:** I picked the customer and two products (the gift basket at HST 13 % and maple syrup, zero-rated). The live total was $204.85 + HST $16.90 = **$221.75**; posting gave INV-0002, and the server totals matched the screen.
2. **Print or download:** I rendered the PDF and checked it visually. Accents print correctly.
3. **New sales receipt:** a debit-card payment deposited to 1010 Chequing: tour × 4 + maple butter = $84.50 + HST $9.36 = **$93.86**. It became SR-0001, marked Paid.
4. **Receive payment** from the list on both invoices. They show Paid, payments appear as Closed rows, and the money bar moved to $0 unpaid / $660.54 received.
5. **Customers and products:** checked both, including opening the side panel from **+ New → Customer**.
6. **Phone width:** no sideways scrolling; the line grid scrolls inside its own box.

Bugs found by the walk-through and fixed:
- the money bar didn't refresh after a payment;
- empty segments were drawn in their alert colour;
- status pills wrapped;
- the company name pushed the account button off the top bar on phones.

### Tests
`sales.test.ts` covers:
- products & services (validation, duplicates, deactivation);
- sales-receipt posting (Dr bank 56.50 / Cr revenue 50.00 / Cr HST 6.50), numbering, void, draft editing, deposit-account rules, and bookkeepers saving drafts but not posting;
- sales receipts in the GST/HST report (line 103, all checks green) and in revenue by channel;
- the new invoice fields and draft editing;
- All sales statuses and filters, money-bar totals, customer balances;
- PDFs, including a 404 for another company's documents.
The route-permission snapshot gained 16 routes (reviewed), and the tenant-isolation and role-matrix tests cover them.

### Not included (unchanged from the plan)
- Sending documents by e-mail; online card payments; inventory quantities; estimates; recurring invoices; Undeposited Funds / bank deposits; custom templates and logos.
- Vendor-side screens (bills, expenses) keep their previous layout inside the new shell; they could get the same treatment next.
