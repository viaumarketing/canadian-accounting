### 2. Year-end close
- An accountant or owner closes a fiscal year. The system posts one closing journal per year: every revenue and expense account is brought to zero against **Retained earnings**, dated the last day of the year.
- The year's periods are locked. Closing is refused if:
  - the year is already closed;
  - an earlier year is still open;
  - draft entries exist in the year.
  A year with no revenue or expenses closes without a journal.
- **Reopen** posts the exact reversal of the closing entry. Periods stay locked: you unlock only the ones you need to adjust, which is safer than unlocking a whole year. It is audited and needs `journal:reverse`.
- The balance sheet stops showing "net income not yet closed" for closed years, and the income statement for a closed year excludes the closing entry, so it still shows the year's results.
- Tests: balance sheet before and after close equal in total; trial balance balanced; P&L unchanged; posting into a closed year refused; reopen restores the state.

### 3. Quick Method of accounting for GST/HST
- Per-registration setting: method (regular / quick), effective date, and the Quick Method remittance rate the business is eligible for. Rates are entered by the user and **not hard-coded**: CRA publishes them by province and business type, and they change.
- Return support follows CRA's published guide for the Quick Method (checked against canada.ca before coding):
  - line 101 sales;
  - line 103 tax collected;
  - line 106 ITCs limited to capital property;
  - line 107 adjustments;
  - the 1 % credit on the first eligible supplies, if verified;
  - the remittance computed as rate × GST/HST-included eligible supplies.
- Anything that cannot be verified on canada.ca is left as a user-entered amount and labelled as such.
- Tests with worked examples from the CRA guide, if it provides any; otherwise hand-computed examples that are clearly marked as such.

### 4. Operations
- **Off-NAS backup copy:** optional `BACKUP_COPY_DIR`, a second folder (for example a USB drive or another NAS share mounted in UGOS). - **TOTP replay protection:** a code that was already accepted for a user cannot be used again within its validity window (stored as the last accepted time step).
- **Paginated general ledger:** the JSON view is paged (opening balance carried forward, page size limited); exports stay complete.
- **Cash-flow classification screen:** set each account's cash-flow class (operating / investing / financing / default) in the chart-of-accounts UI; audited; needs `accounts:create`.

### Quick Method (Taxes → GST/HST Quick Method; GST/HST report)
- Rules taken from CRA guide **RC4058 (Rev. 25)** on canada.ca:
  - **line 101:** eligible sales including GST/HST, rounded to the dollar per remittance rate;
  - **line 103:** Σ sales × remittance rate, plus GST/HST in full on supplies with no rate;
  - **line 106:** ITCs on capital and real property (you enter), plus the credit on 0%-rate supplies;
  - **line 107:** the 1 % credit on the first $30,000 of eligible sales per fiscal year, carried across the year's periods, and only if the election was in effect at the start of the fiscal year (or the registration date for new registrants), plus adjustments.
- **Rates:** recorded per sales tax code by the user and never hard-coded. Codes without a rate count as non-eligible supplies.
- **Suggested journal:** the report includes the entry that moves the Quick Method difference into income. It is **not** posted.
- **Tests** (`quick-method.test.ts`):
  - reproduces CRA's worked example exactly: 103 = 1,584; 106 = 252; 107 = 300; net tax = 1,032;
  - the 1 % credit across quarters;
  - an election starting mid-year (no credit; a period straddling the start date is flagged);
  - non-eligible codes;
  - validation, permissions, audit, and switching back to the regular method.
- **Limits:**
  - GST/HST from Shopify orders and bank receipts can't be split by remittance rate automatically. The report flags it, and you enter those sales as "other eligible sales" with their rate.
  - Supplies made in both participating and non-participating provinces follow the per-tax-code rates you record.
  - Public service bodies' special quick method (RC4247) is not supported.

### Bug found in the test harness (fixed): failing tests did not fail CI
- **Symptom:** a failing test still made `vitest` (and so `pnpm test` and CI) exit with code 0.
- **Cause:** `embedded-postgres`, the database the tests start, registers `async-exit-hook`. Its `beforeExit` handler calls `process.exit(0)` and replaced vitest's failure code; its `exit` handler throws.
- **Effect:** CI had been green only because every test actually passed. A regression would not have stopped a release.
- **Fix:** `test/global-setup.ts` removes those two listeners (teardown still stops the database).
- **Verified:** a deliberately failing test now exits 1, a passing suite exits 0, and no test database is left running.
