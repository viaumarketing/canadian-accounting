# 1.0.1 — QuickBooks-class feature set and SaaS edition

Status: **implemented and verified** on a workstation (automated tests, production build, browser walk-through); external services not verified live. See Results.

## Decisions (from the product owner)
- **Card payments:** Stripe. The same provider handles SaaS subscriptions (Stripe Billing).
- **Editions:** one codebase, two modes. `APP_MODE=selfhosted` (UGREEN NAS; no billing, everything enabled) and `APP_MODE=saas` (public sign-up, subscriptions, plan limits).
- **Extra features:** bills & purchase orders; statements & reminders; projects & time; budgets & class/location tracking. **Payroll is out of scope**: Canadian payroll needs CRA source-deduction tables, T4/ROE filing and certification.
- **Release:** PRs #1 and #2 merged into `main`; 1.0.1 is built on `release/1.0.1`, merged to `main` and tagged `v1.0.1`.

## Phases

| # | Phase | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | E-mail & branding | **Resend** e-mail service with a per-company sender and reply-to and a delivery log. E-mail invoices, sales receipts, estimates and statements with the PDF attached. **Logo upload** and **document templates**: accent colour, layout options, which fields to show, footer and payment instructions. Templates apply to PDFs and e-mails. |
| 2 | Estimates, recurring, statements, reminders | **Estimates:** create, send, accept/decline, convert to an invoice. **Recurring transactions:** invoices, bills and expenses on daily/weekly/monthly/yearly schedules, either creating drafts or posting and sending, run by the worker. **Customer statements:** balance-forward and open-item, as PDF or e-mail. **Automatic overdue reminders:** a schedule per company. Optional **late fees**. |
| 3 | Card payments & Undeposited Funds | Per-company Stripe connection (the company's own keys, encrypted), a **Pay now** link on invoices (Stripe Checkout), and a webhook with signature verification. Payments post to a **Stripe clearing** account; fees and payouts follow the same clearing pattern as Shopify. **Undeposited Funds** account, with payments and receipts able to land there. **Bank deposit** screen groups undeposited items (plus other lines and fees) into one deposit. |
| 4 | Inventory | **Inventory items:** quantity on hand, inventory asset account, COGS account, reorder point. Purchases (bills) increase quantity and value at cost. Sales post COGS at **weighted-average cost**, which ASPE allows. **Inventory adjustments** for quantity and value, a low-stock list, and Inventory Valuation Summary/Detail reports. |
| 5 | Bills & purchase orders | **Purchase orders** (no ledger entry) with e-mail and convert to a bill. **Pay bills:** many bills, one payment. Supplier credits applied to bills. Recurring bills and expenses come from phase 2. |
| 6 | Projects & time | **Projects:** customer, status, budget. **Timesheets:** user, date, hours, service, billable rate. Billable expenses on bill and expense lines. **Add billable time and expenses to an invoice.** Project profitability report. |
| 7 | Budgets & tracking | **Classes** and **locations** on document and journal lines. P&L by class and by location. **Budgets** by account and month for a fiscal year, with a budget-vs-actual report. Dashboard charts: income and expenses by month, A/R aging, bank balances, profit and loss. |
| 8 | SaaS edition | **Organizations** (the subscribing business, owning one or more companies). **Public sign-up** with e-mail verification, a 30-day trial, and **plans** that limit users, companies and features. Stripe Checkout for subscriptions, the customer portal, and webhooks that keep subscription status in sync. Read-only mode when a subscription lapses. Landing and pricing pages. A platform console for subscribers and subscriptions. Self-hosted mode skips all of it. |
| 9 | Release | Brand-new README, version 1.0.1 in every package, CHANGELOG, full verification (lint, typecheck, tests, migration check, audit, image build), merge to `main`, tag `v1.0.1` (the release workflow publishes the images). |

## Rules carried over
- Every posting goes through the ledger's single posting path; posted entries stay immutable; voids are reversals.
- Every migration is additive (CI-enforced), so a rollback stays app-only.
- Every new company-scoped route gets a permission, and the generated tenant-isolation and role-matrix tests cover it automatically; the route snapshot is reviewed.
- External APIs (Resend, Stripe) are built from their **official documentation**, tested against faithful fakes, and listed as **not verified live** until your test keys are used.

## Not in 1.0.1
Payroll; Stripe Connect (each company brings its own Stripe account instead); multi-currency; FIFO inventory; mobile apps; bank-feed providers beyond Plaid.

### Database
One migration, `20261108000000_v101_features`: 724 lines generated by `prisma migrate diff` against a shadow PostgreSQL 17,
then reviewed. No drops and no column changes. New enum *types* only (estimate and PO status); no new values on existing
enums, because an older Prisma client cannot read an unknown enum value, and that would break a rollback. `SCHEMA_COMPAT_LEVEL`
is unchanged, so 0.9.2 still runs on the upgraded database.

### Walk-through in the browser (dev database, Québec company)
Dashboard charts (12 months, A/R aging, bills owed, bank balances); new estimate with a product → EST-0001 → converted to
a draft invoice (lines and memo carried over) → posted INV-0003; the Send panel, which shows the "e-mail not set up"
warning because dev has no Resend key; Branding tab; inventory product created → bill for 24 units at $4.50 (account
locked to 1200 Inventory) → valuation $108.00 "agrees with the ledger"; bank deposit, budgets (fiscal-year grid April to
March) and projects pages; the customer invoice link at phone width (no Pay button because Stripe is not connected). PDFs (three
invoice layouts with a logo, purchase order, statement) were rendered to images and checked by eye.

Bugs found by the walk-through and fixed:
- The e-mail preview filled `{{link}}` with explanatory text, which would have been sent instead of the real link. The
  placeholder is now kept until sending, and a test covers the round trip.
- An empty link left a "View it online:" line without a URL.
- "Send reminder" appeared on invoices that were not overdue.
- The bill editor had no way to add a supplier.
- Payment and deposit account lists filtered by account-code prefixes, which would hide accounts in custom charts.
- The inventory adjustment defaulted to an equity account.
